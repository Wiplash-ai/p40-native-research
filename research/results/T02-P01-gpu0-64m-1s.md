## Interpretation

At 125 W, this simple large streaming-copy path reached about 84% of the P40's
346 GB/s SKU memory-bandwidth figure under algorithmic read+write accounting.
That is encouraging for bandwidth-bound low-bit kernels, but it does not show
effective DRAM transactions, copy-engine bandwidth, DP4A performance, or
Qwen inference speed. The sparse `nvidia-smi` utilization samples are not a
meaningful utilization measurement for a one-second kernel run.
